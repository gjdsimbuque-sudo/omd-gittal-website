About OMD-GiTTaL

AI-driven marketing and automation for multicultural UK and European SMEs.

Open Minded D-GiTTaL Marketing Ltd (trading as OMD-GiTTaL) is a Manchester-based, remote-first agency helping small and medium businesses access the same digital systems larger firms rely on.

Accessible growth for diverse communities

Trusted, culturally fluent marketing for SMEs

  • AI automation and digital marketing for UK and European clients
  • Remote-first support that removes geographic barriers
  • Built for British, African, Lusophone and Spanish-speaking communities

Why we exist

OMD-GiTTaL Marketing was created to close the gap between affordable digital marketing and the needs of smaller businesses.

We make powerful AI automation, web systems and campaign design accessible for firms who want growth without the overhead or complexity of traditional agencies.

“Our goal is simple: remove the barriers that stop SMEs from using modern digital tools, and deliver marketing systems that feel trusted, clear and effective.”

— Founder, OMD-GiTTaL
